\section{Backend utilities reference}
\label{sec:backend:utils}

This section summarizes per-function responsibilities for the key helper modules under \texttt{timetabling\_system/utils} and \texttt{timetabling\_system/services} not covered elsewhere.

\subsection{Venue matching and stats}
\label{subsec:backend:utils:venue}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.36\textwidth}p{0.58\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Function} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{venue\_supports\_caps} & Validates that a venue has all required provision capabilities. \\
\texttt{venue\_has\_timing\_conflict} & Detects overlapping \texttt{ExamVenue} slots for a venue. \\
\texttt{venue\_is\_available} & Checks a venue's availability list for a date. \\
\texttt{attach\_placeholders\_to\_venue} & Binds placeholder \texttt{ExamVenue} rows to a newly capable venue. \\
\texttt{examvenue\_student\_counts} & Aggregates student counts per \texttt{ExamVenue}. \\
\texttt{core\_exam\_size} & Estimates core exam size excluding extra-time allocations. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Excel parsing and classification}
\label{subsec:backend:utils:excel}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.36\textwidth}p{0.58\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Function} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{parse\_excel\_file} & Main Excel parser; classifies and returns structured rows. \\
\texttt{prepare\_exam\_provision\_df} & Normalizes and cleans DataFrames for exam/provision parsing. \\
\texttt{validate\_required\_columns} & Checks column requirements from \texttt{file\_definitions}. \\
\texttt{detect\_provision\_file} & Detects provision files by column signatures. \\
\texttt{detect\_exam\_file} & Detects exam files by column signatures. \\
\texttt{detect\_venue\_file} & Detects structural venue sheets. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Column normalization}
\label{subsec:backend:utils:columns}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.36\textwidth}p{0.58\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Function} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{normalize} & Normalizes column labels to lowercase, underscore-separated tokens. \\
\texttt{map\_equivalent\_columns} & Maps messy headers to canonical names. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Venue parsing and ingest}
\label{subsec:backend:utils:venues}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.36\textwidth}p{0.58\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Function} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule

\texttt{\_cell\_to\_date\_text} & Converts Excel date cells into ISO strings. \\
\texttt{upsert\_venues} & Upserts venues from parsed venue files with defaults. \\
