\section{Glossary and module map}
\label{sec:glossary}

This section defines key domain terms and maps the most important modules to their responsibilities.

\subsection{Glossary}
\label{subsec:glossary:terms}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.68\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Definition} \\
\midrule
\texttt{Diet} & An exam period window (e.g., April/May) with dates and restriction cutoffs. \\
\texttt{ExamVenue} & Join model connecting an exam to a venue and timing context. \\
\texttt{Provision} & A student requirement (extra time, separate room, assistive tech). \\
\texttt{Placeholder ExamVenue} & An \texttt{ExamVenue} with no venue assigned yet. \\
\texttt{Capability} & A venue feature used to satisfy provisions (e.g., computer, accessible hall). \\
\texttt{Invigilator} & Staff profile that can be assigned to exam venues and shifts. \\
\texttt{Assignment} & A scheduled invigilator shift tied to an exam venue. \\
\texttt{Restriction} & Invigilator availability limitations for a given diet. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
