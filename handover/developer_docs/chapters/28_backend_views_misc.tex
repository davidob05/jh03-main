\section{Django views, URLs, and throttling}
\label{sec:backend:views}

This section documents the remaining Django views and routing that are not part of the REST API.

\subsection{timetabling\_system views}
\label{subsec:backend:views:timetabling}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.36\textwidth}p{0.58\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{View/Function} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{HomePageView} & Static landing page for the timetabling system. \\
\texttt{AboutPageView} & Static about page for the timetabling system. \\
\texttt{healthz\_view} & Health check endpoint that verifies DB connectivity. \\
\texttt{upload\_timetable\_file} & Form-based upload endpoint for authenticated users (legacy). \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{pages app views}
\label{subsec:backend:views:pages}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.36\textwidth}p{0.58\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{View/Function} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{pages.HomePageView} & Static home page for the pages app. \\
\texttt{pages.AboutPageView} & Static about page for the pages app. \\
\texttt{pages.healthz\_view} & Health check endpoint (pages app). \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{URL routing}
\label{subsec:backend:views:urls}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{timetabling\_system/urls.py} wires home/about/healthz/upload views.
  \item \texttt{pages/urls.py} wires pages app home/about/healthz views.
  \item \texttt{django\_project/urls.py} mounts both and the REST API.
\end{itemize}
