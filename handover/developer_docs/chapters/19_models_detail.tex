\section{Model detail reference}
\label{sec:models:detail}

This section expands on Section~\ref{sec:models} with per-class notes and behavioral constraints for \texttt{timetabling\_system/models.py}.

\subsection{Choice types}
\label{subsec:models:detail:choices}

All choice classes are \texttt{models.TextChoices} used for validation and UI selection. Key types:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{ProvisionType}: normalized provision labels used in ingestion and matching.
  \item \texttt{ExamVenueProvisionType}: capability labels stored on venues and exam venues.
  \item \texttt{VenueType}, \texttt{ExamTypeChoices}, \texttt{DietChoices}, \texttt{SlotChoices}.
  \item \texttt{InvigilatorQualificationChoices}, \texttt{InvigilatorRestrictionType}, \texttt{AccessibilityFeatures}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Core tables}
\label{subsec:models:detail:core}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.30\textwidth}p{0.64\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Notes} \\
\midrule
\texttt{Exam} & Core exam metadata; \texttt{exam\_id} is the primary key. \\
\texttt{Venue} & Primary key is \texttt{venue\_name}; capabilities and availability are stored in arrays. \\
\texttt{ExamVenue} & Join model between exam and venue; \texttt{core} marks primary allocations. \\
\texttt{Student} & Primary key is \texttt{student\_id}; minimal identity data. \\
\texttt{StudentExam} & Unique per (student, exam). On save, validates separate-room exclusivity. \\
\texttt{Provisions} & Stores normalized provision list, notes, and optional extra-time details. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Notifications and uploads}
\label{subsec:models:detail:notifications}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.30\textwidth}p{0.64\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Notes} \\
\midrule
\texttt{Notification} & Stores admin/invigilator messages; ordered newest-first. \\
\texttt{Announcement} & Admin-authored messages with audience and expiry controls. \\
\texttt{UploadLog} & Records file ingest history (created/updated counts). \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Invigilator management}
\label{subsec:models:detail:invigilators}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.30\textwidth}p{0.64\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Notes} \\
\midrule
\texttt{Invigilator} & Profile linked to auth user (optional). \texttt{preferred\_name} used in display. \\
\texttt{InvigilatorQualification} & Unique per invigilator/qualification. \\
\texttt{InvigilatorRestriction} & Diet-scoped restrictions per invigilator. \\
\texttt{InvigilatorAvailability} & Availability by date and slot; unique on (invigilator, date, slot). \\
\texttt{InvigilatorAssignment} & Shift record with timing, role, cancellation, and cover tracking. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Behavioral hooks}
\label{subsec:models:detail:hooks}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{StudentExam.clean()} enforces separate-room exclusivity per \texttt{ExamVenue}.
  \item \texttt{StudentExam.save()} calls \texttt{full\_clean()} to ensure validation.
  \item Venue-related behaviors are augmented by signals (see Section~\ref{sec:signals}).
\end{itemize}
