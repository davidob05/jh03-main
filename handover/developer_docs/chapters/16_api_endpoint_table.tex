\section{API endpoint table}
\label{sec:api:endpoint-table}

This section lists the primary API endpoints, their handlers, and access requirements. It complements the high-level overview in Section~\ref{sec:api}.

\subsection{Authentication}
\label{subsec:api:endpoints:auth}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.30\textwidth}p{0.26\textwidth}p{0.36\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Endpoint} & \textbf{Access} & \textbf{Handler} \\
\midrule
\texttt{POST /api/auth/token/login/} & Public & \texttt{ObtainAuthTokenView} \\
\texttt{GET /api/auth/me/} & Authenticated & \texttt{CurrentUserView} \\
\texttt{PATCH /api/auth/me/} & Authenticated & \texttt{CurrentUserView} \\
\texttt{DELETE /api/auth/me/} & Admin only & \texttt{CurrentUserView} \\
\texttt{GET /api/auth/sessions/} & Authenticated & \texttt{SessionListView} \\
\texttt{POST /api/auth/sessions/revoke/} & Authenticated & \texttt{SessionRevokeView} \\
\texttt{POST /api/auth/sessions/revoke-others/} & Authenticated & \texttt{SessionRevokeOthersView} \\
\texttt{POST /api/auth/logout/} & Authenticated & \texttt{SessionLogoutView} \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
