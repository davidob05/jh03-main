\section{API views reference}
\label{sec:api:views}

This section provides a high-level reference to \texttt{timetabling\_system/api/views.py}. It lists key helpers and view classes with their responsibilities.

\subsection{Helper functions}
\label{subsec:api:views:helpers}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.34\textwidth}p{0.60\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Helper} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{log\_notification} & Writes a \texttt{Notification} row for admin/invigilator events. \\
\texttt{\_get\_request\_user} & Resolves a request user, including serializer context. \\
\texttt{\_resolve\_invigilator\_for\_user} & Maps a user to an \texttt{Invigilator} profile if present. \\
\texttt{\_has\_time\_conflict} & Detects time overlaps between assignments. \\
\texttt{\_format\_dt}, \texttt{\_csv\_for} & CSV formatting helpers for exports. \\
\texttt{\_provision\_row} & Builds a provision allocation row for the student/provision API. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Permission helpers}
\label{subsec:api:views:permissions}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.34\textwidth}p{0.60\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Permission} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{IsInvigilatorOrAdmin} & Allows admin users or authenticated invigilators. \\
\texttt{IsSeniorAdmin} & Restricts access to senior admins. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Export and upload views}
\label{subsec:api:views:exports}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.34\textwidth}p{0.60\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{View} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{ProvisionExportView} & CSV export of provision allocations (admin-only). \\
\texttt{InvigilatorTimetableExportView} & CSV export of invigilator timetables (admin-only). \\
\texttt{TimetableUploadView} & Accepts Excel uploads and triggers ingestion. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{CRUD viewsets}
\label{subsec:api:views:crud}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.34\textwidth}p{0.60\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Viewset} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{ExamViewSet} & Admin CRUD for exams with bulk-delete. \\
\texttt{VenueViewSet} & Admin CRUD for venues with bulk-delete and write serializer. \\
\texttt{ExamVenueViewSet} & Admin CRUD for exam-venue links (core rows are protected). \\
\texttt{InvigilatorViewSet} & Admin CRUD for invigilators plus admin promotion actions. \\
\texttt{InvigilatorAssignmentViewSet} & Admin CRUD and invigilator self-service actions for shifts. \\
\texttt{AnnouncementViewSet} & Admin CRUD for announcements; read for invigilators. \\
\texttt{DietViewSet} & Admin CRUD for diets with cleanup on delete. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{Notes}
\label{subsec:api:views:notes}

The views module currently contains duplicated definitions for \texttt{DietViewSet} and \texttt{\_provision\_row}. If you modify one, ensure both copies remain consistent or refactor to a single source.
